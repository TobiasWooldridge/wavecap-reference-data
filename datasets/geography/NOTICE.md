# Geographic attribution and licence scopes

ABS: Copyright Commonwealth of Australia (Australian Bureau of Statistics),
CC BY4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Source and limitations
are in catalog.json.

OpenStreetMap-derived databases: Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors,
ODbL1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Attribution:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Retain these databases separately
and make the derived database available under ODbL when required. Containing
ABS locality labels additionally retain their CC BY4.0 attribution.

SA retirement-village references: Government of South Australia, CC BY4.0.
DEW CFS map-book references: Government of South Australia, Department for
Environment and Water, CC BY4.0; original archive CC_BY.txt retained.

No dataset identifies incident positions or joins locations to radio IDs.
